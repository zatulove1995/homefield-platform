Quick answers

Three things everyone asks.

Do I need design work?

No. Every industry gets a set of proven templates — aging roof, renewal window, storm follow-up, just-listed-nearby — set in your name, colors, phone number and license line at onboarding. You can edit the headline and the offer on any trigger; you never open a design tool or upload a file.

Is there a minimum?

None. Cards mail one at a time as homes cross your trigger — one card this Tuesday, four hundred the week after a storm. There's no drop size to fill, no campaign to schedule, and no charge for weeks when nothing crosses the trigger.

Who prints and mails the cards?

We do. Printing, addressing, first-class postage and mailing are all included in the 79¢ — the card is typically in the mailbox about three days after the trigger fires. Addresses are verified before print, and any card that comes back undeliverable suppresses that home from future runs automatically.
