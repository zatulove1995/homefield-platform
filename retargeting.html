Three questions, answered.

Do I need a Meta ad account?

Yes — your own. Retargeting runs from your ad account and your business page; Homefield maintains the audience inside it. If you don't have one yet, setup takes about ten minutes during onboarding.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta. The subscription covers the sync; the ad dollars stay between you and your ad account, at whatever budget you set. Homefield never touches the spend and never marks it up.

Can homeowners tell it's retargeting?

No — it reads as a normal sponsored post from your business page, because that's exactly what it is. The only difference is who's in the audience and when: homes in their moment, the week your email and card arrive.
