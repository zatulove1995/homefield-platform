The signals engine

275M+ homes, scored for the moment.

Every channel fires off the same four signals — so a home hears from you when it's ready, not when a list vendor sold its address.

  • 01

    Renewal window

    Modeled from closing anniversaries — most policies bind at purchase, so the renewal window is a date we can see coming.

  • 02

    Roof & system age

    Build year plus permit history — roofs past year 18 and HVAC past year 12, flagged before the breakdown call.

  • 03

    Equity & tenure

    Years in the home and equity accrued — the two numbers that precede almost every listing.

  • 04

    Storm activity

    Hail and wind events mapped to rooftops, matched to homes old enough to have real damage.

FAQ

Straight answers

What's managed and what's self-serve?

Managed Email is done for you: we run warmed-domain campaigns in your business's name, read every reply, and deliver qualified leads — billed per qualified lead, rate quoted by industry, one business per industry per ZIP. The Platform is self-serve: the same signals engine driving SMS follow-up, triggered postcards and Facebook retargeting from one dashboard, open to any business.

Do I need all four channels?

No. Most businesses start with one — usually managed email — and add channels as the moment proves out. They stack: the email lands, the card arrives, the ad follows, same home, same week, so each channel makes the others convert better.

Is the SMS compliant?

Yes — because it's consent-based follow-up, not cold texting. Sequences only go to homeowners who replied or opted in, every message honors opt-outs automatically, and 10DLC registration is handled for you.
