Division of labor

Our column is long. Yours is one line.

Managed email is a service, not software — there's no dashboard to learn and no sequence builder to babysit. Here's the entire split.

  • Domains — warmed sending domains that carry your business name; your own domain is never touched
  • Copy — a personal three-email sequence, approved by you word for word
  • Sending — paced daily volume, deliverability watched send by send
  • Reply grading — every reply read; only real interest is passed on and billed
  • Compliance — CAN-SPAM, physical address, suppression lists, one-click unsubscribes

You handle

Answer qualified leads within a day.

That's the entire column. The homeowner has already told you what they want — a quote, an estimate, a valuation, an offer — in their own words. You reply like a person and take it from there.

Businesses that follow up inside a day report 20–25% close rates. Speed is your only job — we handle everything upstream of it.

The sequence

Three emails over fourteen days. A reply ends it instantly.

Personal, plain-text notes — no templates dressed up as newsletters, no images, no "Dear Homeowner." Each one reads like a short email from a local business, because it is one.

  • Day 0Email one. Names the moment — the renewal window, the roof's age, the equity. One ask, no pitch.
  • Day 6Email two. A shorter nudge from a different angle, for the ones who meant to reply and didn't.
  • Day 14Email three. The last note. Offers an easy no — which is also what makes the yeses easy.
  • Any dayA reply exits the sequence instantly. A homeowner who writes back never receives another automated send.

What you pay for

Qualified replies. Nothing else.

Every reply is read and graded before it reaches you. The bar for "qualified" is written down, and the invoice follows it — every billed lead arrives with its full reply thread attached.

Billed — a qualified lead

  • Asks for a quote, an estimate, a valuation, or an offer
  • Asks what you'd charge, or what the home is worth
  • Asks to be called, or asks for the next step

Never billed

  • Bounces and auto-replies
  • Unsubscribes and opt-outs
  • Wrong contacts
  • Hostile replies
  • A polite "no thanks"

Questions

Three quick answers.

What does the AI actually do?

Four concrete things. Homefield AI watches 275M+ homes and scores 40+ signals daily to pick the moment; it times every send to the home; it writes per-address copy that names the real street and context; and it grades every reply — qualified or not — which is how billing stays honest. It never sends without you: you approve every sequence word for word first.

Will this touch my own email domain?

No — it's never touched. Sending runs on separate warmed domains we own and manage that carry your business name, so the homeowner is talking to you from the first sentence while your actual domain's reputation stays walled off entirely. Your own email keeps working exactly as it does today.

Is this CAN-SPAM compliant?

Yes, and compliance is on us, not you. Every message identifies the real sender, carries a physical mailing address, and honors one-click unsubscribes immediately. We don't send to contacts on suppression or opt-out lists — and opt-outs are suppressed permanently across the network.
