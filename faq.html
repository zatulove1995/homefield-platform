FAQ

Straight answers. All of them.

Every question businesses ask before they start — the managed service, the software channels, compliance, billing — grouped so you can skip to the part you're weighing. If yours isn't here, it's a fifteen-minute call away.

Getting started

What's managed and what's self-serve?

Managed Email is the done-for-you service: we run the campaigns end to end — warmed domains carrying your name, sequences you approve word for word, every reply read and graded — and you're billed per qualified lead, at a rate quoted by industry. The platform is the software side: SMS, postcards, and Facebook retargeting, driven by the same signals engine but by you — you pick the segments, set the triggers, and press the buttons. That's a subscription plus usage, not per-lead.

Which businesses does Homefield serve?

Four kinds. Home services businesses — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, remodeling and similar trades. Home insurance agencies. Real estate agents. Real estate investors. All four buy the same thing: attention from homeowners at the moment they're actually ready. Managed email is territorial — one business per industry per ZIP, so a roofer and an agent can hold the same ZIP; two roofers cannot. The platform channels aren't territorial and are open to any business.

Do I need all four channels?

No. Most businesses start with managed email alone and add channels once the replies are flowing. The channels are built to stack: the email lands, the postcard arrives, the retargeting ad follows — same home, same week — so each one you add makes the others convert better. But nothing requires the bundle; every channel stands on its own.

How fast can I be live?

Managed email: about 21 days to the first qualified lead — roughly two weeks warming sending infrastructure (pre-warmed domain pools shorten this) and building your list, then replies start in the first sending week. The platform: same day. Connect your account, build a segment from your filters, and SMS sequences, postcard triggers, or an audience sync can be running that afternoon.

Managed email

What exactly counts as a qualified lead?

A homeowner in your territory who replies to our outreach expressing interest — asking for a quote, an estimate, a valuation or an offer, asking what you'd charge, asking to be called. Every reply is read and graded — qualified or not — before it reaches your invoice.

What never gets billed?

Auto-replies, bounces, unsubscribes, wrong contacts, and "no thanks" — none of them ever appear on an invoice. You pay only for replies where a homeowner asked for something you sell. If a billed lead shouldn't have qualified, flag it and we review the thread; if we got it wrong, it comes off the invoice.

What close rate should I expect?

Businesses report 20–25% with fast follow-up. Speed is the variable that matters most: get back within a day and those numbers hold; let a reply sit for a week and it decays like any warm lead does. The full reply thread is attached to every lead, so you read exactly what the homeowner asked before you pick up the phone.

How does territory work?

One business per industry per ZIP. Your ZIPs lock on signature and are never resold while you're active — no other Homefield client in your industry can be sold into them. If you go inactive, the territory releases after 30 days. And every reply generated in your ZIPs is yours forever: full follow-up rights, no expiry, no re-sale, including conversations you re-open months later.

Platform & channels

What does Homefield AI actually do?

Four concrete things. It watches 275M+ homes and scores 40+ signals on each of them daily. It times each touch to the individual home's moment — send-time per home, not per blast. It writes per-address copy, so every email names the real street and the real situation. And it grades every reply, qualified or not — which is how managed email bills honestly. What it never does is act alone: you approve every sequence before anything sends.

Is the SMS channel compliant?

Yes, because of what it is: consent-based follow-up, not cold texting. Sequences go only to homeowners who replied to your outreach or opted in, from a number registered to your business — 10DLC registration is handled inside the platform. Quiet hours are enforced automatically, STOP is honored instantly, and opt-outs are suppressed across every campaign. We don't sell cold SMS, and the platform won't send it.

Is there a postcard minimum?

No. There's no drop, no batch, no 500-card floor. You pick a trigger — a renewal window entering, a roof aging past year 18, a storm crossing your ZIPs, a listing hitting the block — and cards print and mail one at a time as individual homes fire it. Per-card pricing includes postage, and templates are built in, so there's no design work either.

Does retargeting need my own ad account?

Yes. Your homeowner segments sync to your Meta ad account as custom audiences — the ads run from your account, under your name, and the ad spend is yours, paid to Meta directly. What the platform does is keep the audience right: your filters, refreshed as homes enter their moment, so the homeowner sees your ad the same week your email or card lands.

Where do the audiences come from?

The signals engine — 275M+ homeowner records scored for timing: renewal windows modeled from closing anniversaries, roof and system age from build year and permits, equity and tenure, storm activity. You set the filters; the platform keeps the segment current, adding homes as they enter their moment and dropping them as it passes. Every channel draws from the same segments, which is why they land on the same homes in the same week.

Compliance & sending

Is cold email to homeowners legal?

Yes, under CAN-SPAM, which governs commercial email in the US — and compliance is on us, not you. Every message identifies the real sender, carries a physical mailing address, and honors one-click unsubscribes immediately. We don't send to contacts on suppression or opt-out lists.

Will this hurt my business's email domain?

No — it's never touched. Sending runs on separate warmed domains we own and manage that carry your business name, so the homeowner is talking to you from the first sentence while your actual domain's reputation stays walled off entirely. Your own email keeps working exactly as it does today.

What about texting and the TCPA?

The TCPA is exactly why the SMS channel is follow-up only. Texts go to homeowners with a consent basis — they replied to your outreach or opted in — never to a cold list. Every message identifies your business, quiet hours are enforced, and STOP works on the first try, permanently. Postcards, for the record, need none of this: direct mail has no consent requirement, which is part of why it's the widest-reach channel on the platform.

How is the homeowner data sourced?

Public-record ownership data — the same records a title company or a direct-mail house works from — layered with licensed commercial data, matched to verified contact details, then scrubbed against suppression and opt-out lists before anything sends. We filter from 275M+ homeowner records by ZIP, home value, home and system age, ownership length, equity band, renewal window for insurance, and owner-occupancy for investors.

Billing

How is managed email billed?

Monthly, in arrears, for the qualified leads delivered that month, at a per-lead rate quoted by industry. The invoice is itemized — every line is a lead, and every lead has its full reply thread attached, so you can audit what you're paying for in one read.

How is the platform billed?

A monthly subscription plus usage: per-message pricing on SMS, per-card pricing on postcards with postage included, and retargeting audience sync included in the subscription — the ad spend itself runs in your Meta account, paid to Meta. Usage is metered as it happens, so a quiet month costs you the subscription and little else.

Can I set caps?

Yes, on both sides. On managed email, tell us the most leads you can actually work in a month and we pace the sending to it. On the platform, every usage channel takes a monthly budget cap — texts and postcards stop at the line and resume next month. You'll never get a surprise invoice because something performed better than expected.

Can I run everything from one place?

Yes — that's the point of the platform. Segments, SMS, postcards and retargeting run from one dashboard, and managed-email results land in the same place. One calendar of touches per home, one budget, and reporting that shows cost per customer across channels — not four tools with four logins.

Is there a contract?

No. Everything is month to month — pause or stop whenever. On managed email, your territory releases 30 days after you go inactive; the replies you were billed for stay yours forever. On the platform, cancel the subscription and the sending simply stops.
