Pricing FAQ

The billing questions, answered

Can I buy the platform without managed email?

Yes. The platform is self-serve software, open to any business — subscription plus usage, no territory required, no managed service attached. Some businesses start there and add managed email when their ZIPs are open.

Can I run managed email without the platform?

Yes. Managed email is a complete service on its own — data, sending, grading, and delivery are all inside the per-lead rate, with no platform fee for the service itself. The platform is there when you want the same signals firing on more channels.

Is managed email exclusive?

Yes — one business per industry per ZIP, locked on signature, never resold. The software is not exclusive: the platform is available to any business, in any territory. Exclusivity is a property of the service, because the service sends as you.

Is there a cap on what I can spend?

If you want one. Set a monthly cap on either part — or both — and we pace to it: managed sending slows so qualified leads land inside the cap, and triggered channels queue sends rather than overshoot. No surprise invoices because the machine performed better than expected.

Are there contracts?

None. Both parts are month to month — pause or cancel anytime. Your managed-email territory holds for 30 days while paused; after that it releases to the next business in your industry.
