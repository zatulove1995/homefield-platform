03 · Channels

Four channels, selling as one.

When the channels speak together, every touch sells — each one knows what the last one did. Email is the channel we run for you — see managed email. The other three are yours to own and drive — the roofer's texts, the agency's cards, the agent's ads — each a step in the same journeys.

Channels · SMS

The follow-up channel. Never the cold one.

We don't do cold texts, and neither should you. SMS on Homefield goes only to homeowners who replied or opted in — the fastest way to turn a warm reply into a booked estimate, with the compliance handled for you.

  • Automated sequences to homeowners who replied or opted in — consent-aware by design
  • Two-way inbox — pick up any thread by hand the moment it gets interesting
  • 10DLC registration handled — carrier compliance done for you at signup
  • Quiet hours and opt-outs automatic — STOP honored instantly, every time
  • 3¢ a message, billed on usage — no bundles, no breakage

Channels · Postcards

Mail that fires on a signal, not a season.

Choose a trigger — a renewal window opening, a roof aging in, a storm passing through, a listing going up on the street you farm — and cards print and mail themselves. The waste in direct mail was never the card; it was blasting a whole ZIP because it's Tuesday.

  • Triggered by the signal — renewal window, roof age, storm activity, new listing nearby
  • No minimum drops — one card mails when one home is ready
  • No design work — templates in your branding, ready the day you sign up
  • 79¢ a card, postage included — billed only for what mails
